Emulated hand presses (`xr run-sequence` `press` steps) click small UIKit buttons again, and land on turned targets. The target resolver measured UIKit panels with component matrices UIKit had not computed yet, so a panel such as the starter's welcome panel was listed as a box reaching the XR origin (about 1.9 × 3.3 × 5.2 m). IWER routed every reach over that box, the fingertip came down onto the button from above, and pointerdown and pointerup landed on different elements, so no click fired even though the press reported contact. A panel's box also leaves out UIKit content that is hidden or scrolled out of view, and an element partly scrolled out of view is pressed on the part that shows; with less than 1 cm of it in view, such as a row that only touches the edge of its list, it counts as scrolled out of view and is neither listed nor pressed. A step that names such an element, or hidden content, by its UUID gets no target either, instead of the middle of where the element is laid out. Faces of turned objects come from the tighter of the object's own box and its axis-aligned box, and always lie on the axis-aligned box: on a row of keys turned 25°, the face of the axis-aligned box lay in front of the neighbouring key, which got the press. `place` sets a held object straight down onto the middle of a tilted top face instead of above it. A button on a UIKit panel that is itself turned (25° in testing) still misses, because IWER routes the reach over the button's own panel; that needs an IWER change. The `iwsdk-ui` skill and the `iwsdk-dev` skill's iteration reference now have agents confirm a press by the state it changes (`ui inspect` or app state): a `contactMs` of 800 or less means the fingertip touched something, not that the control clicked. They also have agents walk to a control out of reach (`body.stepTo`) as a step of its own before pressing it: a press that walks the body holds the touch past the 800 ms click window.
