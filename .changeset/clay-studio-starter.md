Redesign the starter scene for the VR, AR, and Desktop 3D targets as a clay studio with three numbered stations under a dark welcome panel: dark pieces you can grab, the robot you select, and a ring you can spin in place, each on a plinth with a short label. VR and Desktop 3D set the stations on a lattice floor under a soft gradient sky, with a marble sculpture, a sideboard, and a floor lamp in the background; AR keeps passthrough visible and grounds the stations with soft shadows. The desk environment model, the plant, and the WebXR banner are gone from the scene and the asset catalog. The sky, the floor's fade into it, the contact shadows and the AR shadow floor use the built-in `DomeGradient` (tilted and dithered), `DomeFade`, `ContactShadow` and `ShadowCatcher` components, so they are edited in the scene and the editor rather than in code. The props are built in code in `src/scene-assets/studio.scene-asset.ts` and the background furniture in `src/scene-assets/decor.scene-asset.ts` (`.js` in JavaScript projects), with no DOM access, so they add no model downloads. The bundled compose-scene skill gains `references/environment-recipes.md` with the studio, a default sky and AR passthrough setups. Projects now prefer the dark Horizon color scheme, and every target's desktop view frames the scene head-on from 1 m behind the origin.
